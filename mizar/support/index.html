Mizar — Support

Contact: support@midchat.io · Typical response: within 3 business days · Languages: English, Deutsch

What Mizar does

Paste notes, hours, or a timesheet into ChatGPT. Mizar turns them into a one-page invoice PDF — totals calculated to the cent, VAT per rate, English or German.

  1. Reads your notes and proposes line items.
  2. Checks common invoice fields and asks for missing ones (rates, tax rate, invoice number, addresses, bank details) in one checklist.
  3. Shows a draft table for you to confirm.
  4. Calculates net, VAT per rate, discounts, and total to the cent.
  5. Renders the invoice in English or German.

It is designed not to invent amounts, hours, tax rates, invoice numbers, VAT IDs, IBANs, or addresses — please review every invoice before you send it. Missing fields stay as visible placeholders like [IBAN] and the invoice is marked DRAFT.

How to use it

Install from the ChatGPT Plugin Directory (link after approval), then try:

  • Create an invoice from these notes
  • Turn this timesheet into an invoice with 19% VAT
  • Generate an invoice PDF for this client
  • Rechnung auf Deutsch mit 19 % MwSt

Limitations

  • PDF download links expire after about 60 minutes — ask Mizar to render again for a fresh link.
  • No memory across chats — client defaults aren't saved between conversations.
  • Does not send invoices, take payments, or connect to accounting/banking tools.
  • Human-readable invoice, not XRechnung/ZUGFeRD e-invoice.
  • Not tax advice — does not choose your VAT rate or tax status.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry
No PDF appearedAsk “render it as a PDF file”. If the link says it has expired, ask Mizar to render it again.
Totals look wrongAsk “show the math”. Mizar rounds per line, then tax per rate group.
Keeps asking questionsDeliberate for required fields. Say “make it a DRAFT with placeholders” to proceed.
Didn't activateMention Mizar directly or start with “Create an invoice from…”.
