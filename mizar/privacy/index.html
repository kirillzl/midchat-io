Mizar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 (version 1.1.0, hosted server)

Who we are

Mizar is a plugin for ChatGPT and Codex published by Midchat (“we”, “us”). Privacy contact: privacy@midchat.io.

The short version

  • From version 1.1.0, Mizar uses a Midchat server (mcp.midchat.io) to check invoice fields, calculate totals, and render the invoice PDF.
  • ChatGPT sends that server only the invoice details needed for the document (see below) when Mizar’s tools are used. No account, no sign-in.
  • We do not store invoice details: each request is processed in memory and discarded when the response is sent. We keep no database, no files, and no logs of invoice content.
  • Download links are encrypted and expire after about 60 minutes. The invoice data travels inside the encrypted link; anyone you share a link with can open the invoice until it expires.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train models.
  • Your conversation itself is processed by OpenAI under OpenAI’s terms and privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

What the server receives

When ChatGPT calls a Mizar tool it sends the invoice fields you provided in the conversation: seller and client names and addresses, email (optional), VAT ID / tax number, client PO number, bank details (IBAN, BIC, account holder) or payment instructions, invoice number and dates, line items (description, quantity, unit, price, tax rate), discount, tax note and notes. Mizar’s tools do not ask for or receive your chat history, files, location, or account identifiers.

Mizar does not send invoices, contact your clients, take payments, or connect to accounting or banking tools.

Please don’t email real invoices, bank statements, or full receipts unless needed; blur sensitive values.

Sub-processors and recipients

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting of mcp.midchat.io; compute region Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Vercel is a US company; transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses under Vercel’s DPA.
  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting.
  • OpenAI — processes your ChatGPT conversation under its own terms.

Restricted data

Do not enter card numbers, passwords, health data, or government ID numbers; Mizar does not need them.

Your rights (EU/EEA)

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact privacy@midchat.io. Because we do not store invoice data, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve or delete beyond short-lived host logs and support emails. For ChatGPT conversation data, exercise rights with OpenAI — we cannot access or delete it. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

Changes

We’ll update this page when the product changes, adjust the “Last updated” date, and note material changes in the plugin’s release notes.
