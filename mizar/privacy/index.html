Mizar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

Mizar is a plugin for ChatGPT published by Midchat (“we”, “us”). Privacy contact: privacy@midchat.io.

The short version

  • Mizar’s public package today is primarily skills-only: instructions and small scripts that run inside ChatGPT.
  • For skills-only use, Midchat has no server that receives your chat content. We do not see the files or text you process with the plugin.
  • Your conversation and uploads are processed by OpenAI under OpenAI’s terms and privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • We only process personal data if you contact us (support/privacy email) or visit this website.

What Mizar does with your data inside ChatGPT

When you use Mizar you may paste or upload work notes, hours, timesheets (e.g. CSV), and invoice details such as names, addresses, VAT IDs/tax numbers, and bank details (IBAN). Mizar's instructions tell ChatGPT to structure that into line items, calculate totals and VAT, and produce an invoice (PDF/HTML where ChatGPT can run code, otherwise a print-ready text block). Mizar does not send invoices, contact clients, take payments, or connect to accounting or banking tools.

Whether chats and files are stored, for how long, and whether they are used to improve OpenAI’s models is governed by your ChatGPT account settings and OpenAI’s policies, not by us. Tip: only share data the task actually needs.

Your rights (EU/EEA)

For data we hold (e.g. support emails) you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact privacy@midchat.io. For ChatGPT conversation data, exercise rights with OpenAI — we cannot access or delete it. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

Future hosted / MCP version

We may later offer a separate hosted version (e.g. “Mizar Pro”) that renders invoice PDFs on our own server. That is not part of Mizar today. If it launches, it will have its own listing and this policy will be updated before launch.
