Why it is worth opening

  • Messy notes, clean line items“Workshop prep 4h, workshop 6h” becomes a proper table.
  • It asks instead of guessingMissing rate, tax number, or IBAN? One checklist. Nothing invented.
  • Math to the centNet, VAT per rate, discounts, and the total.
  • English or GermanInvoice or Rechnung, your choice.
  • Safe as a draftMissing fields stay visible as [PLACEHOLDER], and the invoice is marked DRAFT.

How it works

  1. Paste notes, hours, or upload a timesheet CSV.
  2. Confirm the draft table and the totals.
  3. Open the invoice PDF from an encrypted link. It expires after about an hour.

Say it like this

  • make an invoice from my notes
  • turn this timesheet into an invoice with 19% VAT
  • generate an invoice PDF for this client
  • Rechnung auf Deutsch mit 19 % MwSt

What you get

Acme GmbH · 12 h × €110 · 19 % VAT · due in 14 days

Good to know

  • The PDF is rendered by Midchat’s server through a short-lived encrypted link. Invoice details are not stored.
  • No memory across chats — client defaults are not saved between conversations.
  • Does not send invoices, take payments, or connect to accounting or banking tools.
  • A human-readable invoice, not an XRechnung or ZUGFeRD e-invoice.
  • Not tax advice. It does not choose your VAT rate or tax status.

Free. Invoice details go to Midchat’s server (EU, Frankfurt) only to compute and render your invoice, and are not stored. See Privacy · Terms · Support.
