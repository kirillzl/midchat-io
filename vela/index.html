How it works

  1. Attach the PDF and say whether you want Excel or CSV.
  2. Review the preview, the totals check, and the warnings.
  3. Copy the CSV into Excel or Google Sheets.

Say it like this

  • turn this PDF table into a spreadsheet
  • convert this PDF to Excel
  • extract the tables from this PDF as CSV
  • turn this bank statement PDF into a spreadsheet

What you get

Bank statement PDF → pages stitched into one table → statement.csv with ISO dates, plain amounts, and a balance check.

Good to know

  • Works from the PDF’s text. A scan with no text layer is reported, not OCR’d.
  • Values stay as they appear. Unclear cells and ambiguous dates are flagged, not guessed. Totals that do not match are reported, not corrected. Compare the result with your PDF.
  • You get copy-paste CSV (standard or German). A downloadable file only where ChatGPT can run code.
  • No memory across chats. Document content is not stored.
  • Uses Midchat’s hosted tools at vela.mcp.midchat.io, with no sign-in. It does not edit or merge PDFs, convert to Word, upload files, or give tax or accounting advice.
