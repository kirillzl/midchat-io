Lyra — Support

Contact: support@midchat.io · Typical response: within 3 business days · Languages: English, Deutsch

What Lyra does

Paste bullets, notes, or a metrics CSV. Get a short client email plus a one-pager with wins, progress, blockers, risks, and next steps. Tone: direct, no fluff.

  1. Structures your notes into wins, progress, blockers, risks, and next steps.
  2. Asks once for missing owners, dates, or metrics.
  3. Produces a client email draft and a one-pager.

How to use it

Install from the ChatGPT Plugin Directory (link after approval), then try:

  • Write a weekly status report for the client from these notes
  • Turn this update into a client email
  • Friday status report — include risks and next steps

Limitations

  • Designed not to add metrics, owners, or dates you didn’t give; missing ones show as TBD. Please review each draft before sending.
  • Does not send email or post to Slack.
  • No memory across chats; report content is not stored.
  • Uses Midchat’s hosted tools at lyra.mcp.midchat.io (no sign-in); no other integrations.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry
Invented a metricThat shouldn't happen — ask it to remove invented numbers and flag gaps.
Wrong toneSay “more direct / more formal / shorter”.
Didn't activateStart with “Friday status report” or “weekly client update”.

Contact & reporting problems

Email support@midchat.io with: what you asked, what happened, what you expected, and a screenshot (blur real names, addresses, bank details, and receipt totals).
