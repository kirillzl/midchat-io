How it works

  1. Paste this week’s notes, bullets, or metrics.
  2. Confirm the audience, the tone, and anything that is missing.
  3. Copy the email draft and the one-pager into your own tools.

Say it like this

  • write my weekly client update
  • write a weekly status report for the client from these notes
  • turn this update into a client email
  • Friday status report — include risks and next steps

What you get

Subject: Week of Oct 6 — Acme redesign
Wins · Progress · Blockers · Risks · Next steps — then a 5-line email.

Good to know

  • Built not to add metrics, owners, or dates you did not give. Missing ones show as TBD. Review each draft before you send it.
  • Does not send email or post to Slack.
  • No memory across chats. Report content is not stored.
  • Uses Midchat’s hosted tools at lyra.mcp.midchat.io, with no sign-in. No other integrations.
