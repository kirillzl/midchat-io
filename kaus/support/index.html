Kaus — Support

Contact: support@midchat.io · Typical response: within 3 business days · Languages: English, Deutsch

What Kaus does

Paste meeting notes or a transcript. Get a table of owner / action / due date / source quote, plus draft Slack or email follow-ups you send yourself.

  1. Extracts commitments with owner / action / due / source quote.
  2. Flags missing owners and vague language.
  3. Drafts follow-up Slack or email text.

How to use it

Install from the ChatGPT Plugin Directory (link after approval), then try:

  • Extract action items from this meeting
  • Turn this transcript into next steps
  • Draft follow-ups from this call

Limitations

  • Designed not to add owners or deadlines that aren’t in your notes; missing ones show as TBD. Please review the list before sharing it.
  • Does not auto-send messages or create calendar events.
  • No memory across chats; meeting content is not stored.
  • Uses Midchat’s hosted tools at kaus.mcp.midchat.io (no sign-in); no CRM or calendar integrations.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry
Wrong ownerCorrect it in chat; Kaus is designed to use only names from your notes.
No due datesIt shows TBD when the notes don't give a date — that's intentional. Tell it the meeting date to turn “by Friday” into a calendar date.
Didn't activateStart with “extract action items from this meeting”.

Contact & reporting problems

Email support@midchat.io with: what you asked, what happened, what you expected, and a screenshot (blur real names, addresses, bank details, and receipt totals).
