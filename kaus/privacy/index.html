Kaus — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 (version 1.1.0, hosted server)

Who we are

Kaus is a plugin for ChatGPT published by Midchat (“we”, “us”). Privacy contact: privacy@midchat.io.

The short version

  • From version 1.1.0, Kaus uses a Midchat server (kaus.mcp.midchat.io) to find likely action items in your meeting notes, resolve due dates against the meeting date, check the list for missing owners or dates, and lay out the action table and follow-up drafts.
  • ChatGPT sends that server only the meeting content needed for the action list (see below) when Kaus’s tools are used. No account, no sign-in.
  • We do not store meeting content: each request is processed in memory and discarded when the response is sent. We keep no database, no files, and no logs of meeting content.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train models.
  • Your conversation itself is processed by OpenAI under OpenAI’s terms and privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

What the server receives

When ChatGPT calls a Kaus tool it sends content you provided in the conversation: the meeting notes or transcript you pasted (which can include participant names and what they said), the meeting date and timezone if you gave them, the action items (owner, action, due date, source quote), ambiguity and parking-lot notes, an optional meeting title and summary, and an optional sender name for the follow-up drafts. Kaus’s tools do not ask for or receive your chat history, files, location, or account identifiers.

Kaus does not send email or Slack messages, create calendar events or tickets, or connect to your calendar, CRM or project tools.

Tip: only share what the action list needs. Please don’t email confidential meeting notes or transcripts to support unless needed; blur names and sensitive values.

Sub-processors and recipients

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting of kaus.mcp.midchat.io; compute region Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Vercel is a US company; transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses under Vercel’s DPA.
  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting.
  • OpenAI — processes your ChatGPT conversation under its own terms.

Restricted data

Do not enter card numbers, passwords, health data, or government ID numbers; Kaus does not need them.

Your rights (EU/EEA)

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact privacy@midchat.io. Because we do not store meeting content, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve or delete beyond short-lived host logs and support emails. For ChatGPT conversation data, exercise rights with OpenAI — we cannot access or delete it. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

Changes

We’ll update this page when the product changes, adjust the “Last updated” date, and note material changes in the plugin’s release notes.
