How it works

No new tool to learn. You stay in the chat.

1

Say the job

Type it the way you would to a colleague. “Turn this PDF table into a spreadsheet.”

2

The app does the structured part

Line items, owners, VAT, or tables. If something required is missing, it asks — it does not invent it.

3

You take the draft

An invoice, an email, an action list, or a CSV. You send it yourself. Nothing goes out on its own.

Built for the chat, not a new login.

No sign-in

The apps use Midchat’s hosted tools. There is no account to create.

You review it

Totals, owners, and amounts are for you to check. The apps do not send, file, or pay anything.

Not stored

Content is processed only to return the result, and is not stored. Read the privacy page for the app you use.
