Alcor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 (version 1.1.0, hosted server)

Who we are

Alcor is a plugin for ChatGPT published by Midchat (“we”, “us”). Privacy contact: privacy@midchat.io.

The short version

  • From version 1.1.0, Alcor uses a Midchat server (alcor.mcp.midchat.io) to read the date, merchant, total, VAT and currency from receipt text, suggest expense categories, check the rows for gaps and duplicates, and lay out the expense table, totals and CSV.
  • ChatGPT sends that server only the receipt details needed for the expense sheet (see below) when Alcor’s tools are used. No account, no sign-in.
  • We do not store receipt data: each request is processed in memory and discarded when the response is sent. We keep no database, no files, and no logs of receipt data.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train models.
  • Your conversation itself is processed by OpenAI under OpenAI’s terms and privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

What the server receives

When ChatGPT calls an Alcor tool it sends content you provided in the conversation: the text of your receipts as printed or as read by ChatGPT from your photos or PDFs (which can include merchant names and addresses, amounts, VAT, payment method and the last digits of a card), the expense rows built from them (date, merchant, description, category, amounts, currency, business purpose, project, file name), an optional reporting period, home currency and exchange rates you gave, and your own category list if you have one. Alcor’s tools do not receive the receipt images or files themselves, your chat history, location, or account identifiers.

Alcor does not sync to accounting software, submit expense claims, send email, or connect to your bank, card or accounting accounts.

Tip: only share what the expense sheet needs. Please don’t email real receipts or card details to support unless needed; blur names, card numbers and other sensitive values.

Sub-processors and recipients

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting of alcor.mcp.midchat.io; compute region Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Vercel is a US company; transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses under Vercel’s DPA.
  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting.
  • OpenAI — processes your ChatGPT conversation under its own terms.

Restricted data

Do not enter full card numbers, passwords, health data, or government ID numbers; Alcor does not need them (at most the last 4 card digits are kept in a row).

Your rights (EU/EEA)

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact privacy@midchat.io. Because we do not store receipt data, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve or delete beyond short-lived host logs and support emails. For ChatGPT conversation data, exercise rights with OpenAI — we cannot access or delete it. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

Changes

We’ll update this page when the product changes, adjust the “Last updated” date, and note material changes in the plugin’s release notes.
